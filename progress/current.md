### Key discovery: `lg:hidden` vs `flex` cascade conflict

`#mobile-menu-btn` carried both `lg:hidden` and an unprefixed `flex`. In the built CSS,
`.flex{display:flex}` (byte 30679) is emitted **after** `.lg\:hidden{display:none}` (byte 26648),
so the hamburger was visible on desktop at ≥1024px on every page of `origin/master` —
pre-existing, not introduced here. Adding the 9th nav item pushed the homepage to
`scrollWidth 1031 > 1024`. Fix (user-approved): `flex` → `max-lg:flex` on all 6 pages, so the
two rules can never coexist and emission order stops mattering.

Also confirmed: `global.css`'s **unlayered** `*{margin:0;padding:0}` beats Tailwind v4's
`@layer utilities`, so every `margin-*`/`padding-*` utility site-wide is inert, as is `text-*`
on `h1`/`h2`. The new page compensates with a small scoped `<style>` block.
